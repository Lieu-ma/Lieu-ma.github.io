TransSSM — Hybrid Transformer & state-space model

A physics-informed architecture that combines global attention with sequential state-space modeling for solution learning. Evaluated on Reaction, Convection, Wave, and 2D Navier–Stokes cylinder-wake benchmarks.

Scientific ML · 2026Inverse PDEsSparse observations

CABiSSM — Learning hidden PDE coefficients

Developed a cross-attentive bidirectional selective state-space model to reconstruct dense coefficient fields from sparse sensors, evaluated on variable-speed Wave, KdV, and Sine–Gordon inverse problems.
